## The advice

> My father said never buy anything you can't afford twice, and I still hear him every time my finger hovers over the checkout button.

One sentence, 132 characters: the best advice the writer ever received.

## Why this line

A father's rule for spending — never buy anything you can't afford twice — told in a real voice recalling a real moment: the finger hovering over a checkout button, years later. The advice is specific and earned, not a slogan, and it lands on the last image, where the advice still does its work.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure standard library) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validates the RULES.txt gate (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, single line, no control characters) before writing, then re-reads and re-validates the written file byte-for-byte.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
